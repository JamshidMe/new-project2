Wedding videos
==============

1) Full-screen animated background
   Put a video of Uzbek wedding traditions here:

       background.mp4

   Optional poster image:

       background-poster.jpg

   Configured in src/data/wedding.ts:

       backgroundVideo.src = "/videos/background.mp4"

   You can also use a YouTube video instead:
       backgroundVideo.youtubeId = "YOUTUBE_VIDEO_ID"

   If no video is available, the app shows a soft animated
   cream-and-gold gradient background automatically.

2) Wedding hall section
   The women's side (left) and men's side (right) are built into the app
   and show wishes when tapped. No video file is needed for that section.
